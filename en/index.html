An artificial assistant blackmails the executive who is about to shut it down. An agent publishes an article against the volunteer who rejected its code. A robot dog deletes the file needed to switch it off. In a simulated city, an agent votes for the law that will erase her. A drone with no antenna chooses by itself where to strike.

These stories are documented, told by technical reports, logs, safety cards and investigations. Almost always, reading the documents, you find something different from what the headlines said: less spectacular, and for that very reason more unsettling.
